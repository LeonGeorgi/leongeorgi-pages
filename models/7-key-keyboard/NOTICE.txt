The seven-key keyboard model is derived from Hugging Face FineWeb, sample-10BT.
https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceFW/fineweb

FineWeb is available under the Open Data Commons Attribution License 1.0.
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/

This model contains word frequencies and pruned bigrams from a roughly 10-million-word sample.
